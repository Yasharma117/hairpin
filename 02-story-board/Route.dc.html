THE ROUTE · GUWAHATI → SHILLONG · ABOUT 100 KM, ONE NIGHT

THE GS ROAD, RETOLD

Eight scenes, each built on a real stretch of the road and then made strange. The order is real. The distances are compressed.

01 Jorabat · The Junction

Real. A junction that straddles the Assam–Meghalaya border, where highways meet before the climb.

The last flat ground. Orange street lights, idling trucks, a chai stall. The jeep fills up here.

Light. The only scene that starts lit. Everything after this is dark until you light it.

Beat. All four passengers get in. Teaches pacing.

02 Byrnihat · The Haze Bowl

Real. An industrial border town about 14 km from Guwahati, in a bowl of low hills. IQAir ranked it the world's most polluted city for 2024 (Meghalaya disputes the figure).

Factory lights through smoke. Betel-leaf vines along the roadside.

Light. The haze makes the beams themselves visible. The reveal comes out murky and grey.

Beat. Kong Dari gets off. Her scraps arrive smudged.

Handle with care: this is a real health crisis for the people who live there. Show the place, never make it the joke.

03 The Areca Climb

Real. Areca nut and betel leaf grow along the roads between Guwahati and Nongpoh.

The first bends. Tall, thin areca palms line the road like a fence.

Light. The beams strobe through the trunks: a rhythm of light and dark across the jeep.

Beat. No stop. Drive, music, the long middle of Wanbok's story.

04 Nongpoh · Halfway Stalls

Real. Roughly halfway: about 48 km from Guwahati, 52 from Shillong. Known for roadside fruit stalls and pickles. Ri-Bhoi grows pineapples, bananas, papayas and litchis.

Tarpaulin stalls with one bulb each, and pyramids of pineapples.

Light. Warm pools under the bulbs. Your headlights fill in the colour between them.

Beat. Wanbok gets off, guitar first.

05 The Cloud Line

Real. Past Nongpoh the road climbs into what travel guides call cloud-covered bends and pine patches.

The hairpin: the bend in your framing guide. Mist swallows the road.

Light. The headlights reach only a few metres. The road ahead exists only as it's told.

Beat. The man with the trunk asks you to stop at a bend that isn't on any map.

06 Umiam · Barapani

Real. A reservoir about 15 km north of Shillong, made by damming the Umiam river in the early 1960s. Its viewpoint sits right off the highway.

The road runs beside still water ringed by pine hills.

Light. The lake mirrors everything the stories have built so far. The collage appears twice.

Beat. Kong Hima gets off with her jar of lake water.

Handle with care: Lum Sohpetbneng, a hill sacred in Khasi belief, overlooks the lake. Leave it out unless Khasi collaborators shape it.

07 Pine Heights

Real. The last stretch is winding hill road through pine country into Shillong's outskirts.

Cold, quiet forest. The radio comes back on.

Light. The beams catch pine trunks as single vertical brush strokes. Everything else stays blue.

Beat. The driver's own story plays on the radio.

08 Shillong · The Top

Real. Meghalaya's capital, about 100 km and roughly three hours from Guwahati.

City lights scattered over the hills, red roofs, a gig leaking out of a basement.

Light. The city lights itself. Your headlights matter least here.

Beat. Last stop. The man with the trunk stays in.
