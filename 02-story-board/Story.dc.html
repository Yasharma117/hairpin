HAIRPIN · THE STORY · V2 · REPLACES THE PASSENGER ANTHOLOGY

One night, two people, one wish

A taxi from Jorabat to Shillong, about six minutes. It nearly goes over at the cloud line. At the edge you tell the story of the grandfather who went over on this same road and lived. At Shillong a star falls. The whole piece is built around one idea: a wish kept in the bank until you need it.

THE SPINE · TENSION OVER TIME
01 · JORABAT0:00
Who's driving. The passenger gets in and asks "So what do you do?" The answer is the dashboard. Each of the last seven months is an object stuck to it: the teleprompter strip, the 1mg pill strip, a printed test sheet, a billing receipt, a Markon card.
You do. Hover an object and it lights up and gets one line. You don't have to; the drive starts in 40 seconds anyway.
02 · THE CLIMB0:40
How you got here. Robotics in Chennai, the turn to design, the habit of building your own tools. Told in pieces between bends. The world keeps moving: the grass in the wind, cows looking up, the dog chasing.
You do. Drive, honk, and choose which questions to answer. This is the part already built.
03 · NONGPOH1:50
The seed. Rain starts. A throwaway line, said and then dropped: "My grandfather drove this every weekend. Court in Shillong." Nobody follows it up yet.
Why. The payoff at the edge only works if it was set up here. Without it, the story comes out of nowhere.
04 · CLOUD LINE2:30
The slide. The cloud swallows the road. The bend shows up too late. The headlights hit a wall of white and the edge line runs out.
You do. Brake. The ending is fixed, but how long you held the brake decides how far the nose ends up hanging. Your input changes how it happens, not what happens.
05 · THE HANG2:45
Get out. Front wheels over the edge. The taxi seesaws on its belly. The beams point straight out into the cloud and light it from inside.
You do. Open the passenger door first. Their weight leaving tips the nose down a little more. Then your door. You're out just in time, and the car stays hanging there for the rest of the scene.
06 · THE EDGE2:50
Sit. Seen from behind, legs over the drop. The taxi creaks behind them, still lit, rocking in the wind. Nobody talks for four seconds, and that silence is in the edit on purpose. Then: "This happened to my grandfather. Same road."
Camera. Locked. The first held frame in the piece.
07 · THEN3:10
The story. As you tell it, a third painting wipes across the night along the same brush strokes: the same slope, decades earlier, warm and faded. Your grandfather and his friend, every weekend, the court in Shillong. Then the night their car ran off the edge, and they both walked away.
Shader. The reveal mask from the headlights, run as a slow wipe, now to then.
08 · HE SMOKED3:55
The figure. "He sat on the edge, just like this, and lit one." First an ember appears beside you. Then, around it, a man drawn only in ink: no fill, no paint, slower boil than the rest of the frame. The smoke comes off the mist itself.
You do. Nothing. Move the cursor toward him and he thins out. You only see him if you don't look straight at him.
09 · THE ROPE4:35
He goes. The story ends. The ember gets flicked over the edge and falls slowly into the cloud, glowing, and the figure is gone. A truck's horn sounds around the bend. A rope, three men, the taxi hauled back onto the road. One line from the trucker, in Khasi.
You do. Honk back. The horn you've used all night is how you answer.
10 · SHILLONG5:00
The star. At the viewpoint, the city spread out below. The taxi stops, the engine ticks as it cools, and a shooting star crosses. The quote sets itself on torn paper, one line at a time.
You do. Hold the mouse while the star crosses to bank a wish, or don't. Either way, the next time you open the piece the dashboard shows one more object: a small star.
HOW THE FIGURE IS MADE
He isn't in either painting, so the headlights can't reveal him. That's the rule that makes him a ghost, and it costs nothing to explain.
  • Ink only. A seated sprite that writes into the character mask but gets no colour pass. The ink shader draws his outline and nothing fills it.
  • Older line. He boils at 4 fps, everything else at 8. Lighter pressure, more breaks in the line. He's drawn like an older drawing.
  • The ember is the only colour. An additive point that brightens with each drag and casts a faint warm light on the edge of his face.
  • Smoke from the mist. The cloud UV gets a curl pass around the ember, so the smoke and the fog are made of the same stuff.
  • Peripheral. Opacity falls as the cursor gets close. On a phone, it falls as the device tilts toward him.
THE QUOTE · THREE CUTS FROM YOUR MESSAGE
The original's last two lines are addressed to one person. These drop them, so the line belongs to the road, the grandfather and the night instead.
A · CLOSEST TO YOURS
I have the faintest memory of seeing a shooting star. Walking out after dinner, I looked up and saw a streak. I thought of making a wish, and for some reason I didn't. I just smiled. I told myself I had a wish in the bank, for when I needed it.
B · PAYS OFF THE CLIFF (RECOMMENDED)
Years ago I saw one of these and didn't wish. I just smiled, and kept it in the bank for when I'd need it. I think I spent it tonight, at the cloud line. Maybe he spent his on this road too. This one, I'm keeping.
C · SHORTEST
I never made the wish. I kept it in the bank. Tonight, I found out what it was for.
OPEN · NEEDS YOU
  • Who is the other person? A real friend, a stranger who flags the taxi, or the listener (the player)? It changes the dialogue in 01, 02 and 06.
  • Your grandfather. Roughly which decade, what he went to court for (lawyer, judge, a case of his own), his friend's name if you want it used, and what car it was. The "then" painting and the figure's clothes depend on all of this.
  • Which Shillong spot. Pick a viewpoint you'd actually stop at at night.
  • The intro objects. Five projects from the last seven months, one line each. I've guessed: teleprompter, 1mg banner tool, Warden, Dodo billingSDK, Markon.
  • New paintings, lit and dark: the cliff edge, the "then" version of the same slope, the Shillong view. New sprites: the two of you seated from behind, the figure as a line drawing, the truck.
