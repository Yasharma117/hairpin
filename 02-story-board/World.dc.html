WORLD BIBLE · DRAFT 1 · GUWAHATI → SHILLONG, ONE NIGHT

HAIRPIN

The last shared jeep of the night climbs from Guwahati to Shillong. Every passenger tells a story on the way, and the hills build themselves out of what they say.

HOW THE WORLD WORKS

MIST
The monsoon takes the road every night. Past the headlights, nothing exists until someone talks about it.
MATERIAL
Words arrive as paper. Photos, tickets, letters and cassette sleeves fly in and paste themselves onto the blank hills.
PACE
The drive lasts as long as the story. Drive fast and you arrive before the ending. They step out mid-sentence.
RESIDUE
What's told stays. Each night's collage is left on the road, peeling a little, for the next passenger to pass.
