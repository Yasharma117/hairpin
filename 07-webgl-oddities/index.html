WebGL Oddities

Small, strange worlds people built for the browser, and what to take from each for Story Terrain. Every link was opened and its code checked for WebGL or WebGPU.

What the best of these share

  1. A system beats a scene. Napoleon, Orb.farm and AI Town set rules and let you watch. Every visit is different, so people come back.
  2. Data becomes geography. Map of GitHub, Radio Garden and Shipmap give data a place, and people explore it like land. Story Terrain belongs here.
  3. Sound carries half the weight. Shipmap's Bach, Bear 71's narrator, FRONTIER's rule that the soundtrack is the product.
  4. One constraint, owned completely. Susurrus is all watercolour, Sandspiel is all pixels, Napoleon is all sprites.
  5. Small and finished beats big. Most of this list was made by one or two people.
